# Role Contributions are versioned structured proposals

One Role Attempt terminates with a bounded schema-versioned Role Contribution containing hypotheses, Candidate Findings, Coverage Observations, Evidence references and requests, challenges, uncertainty, limitations, Follow-up Requests, resource use, and completion disposition. The Service validates and admits it as a proposal; Markdown narrative and the Role Transcript are derived or diagnostic views. A Role Contribution cannot decide a final Finding state, Technical Severity, Policy Significance, Risk Acceptance, Security Verdict, Seal, or any product mutation.
