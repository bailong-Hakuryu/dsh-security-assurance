# The Kernel freezes Validation Contract Resolution

Before validation work is admitted, the Kernel resolves the exact Validation Contract and version from the Primary Weakness, Security Policy, Assessment Mode, Subject ecosystem, execution boundary, and available qualified validation capabilities. A model, Analyzer, scanner, repository, or caller cannot choose an easier contract after seeing a result. The resolution and alternatives considered are frozen into the validation node; absence of an applicable qualified contract creates a Proof Gap and unresolved Coverage rather than falling back to a generic confidence threshold.
