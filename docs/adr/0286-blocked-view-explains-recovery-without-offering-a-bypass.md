# BLOCKED View explains recovery without offering a bypass

The BLOCKED recovery view identifies each stable blocker code, affected obligation and phase, failed or interrupted Attempt, retained Evidence, remaining eligibility and budget, required external condition, and currently authorized Resume, Cancel, or Risk Decision action. It contains no ignore, force complete, mark covered, change composition, delete failure, produce Verdict, or Seal button. When no eligible path remains it explains Coverage Reconciliation and possible `INDETERMINATE` sealing without allowing the operator to assert completion.
