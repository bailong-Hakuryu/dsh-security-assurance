# Only Coverage Reconciliation can seal INDETERMINATE

A recoverable analyzer, provider, process, or orchestration failure leaves the Assessment BLOCKED. Automatic sealing as INDETERMINATE is permitted only when deterministic Coverage Reconciliation proves and records that no eligible execution path remains under the frozen Coverage Plan or that the frozen Assessment Budget is exhausted. Neither a user, role agent, Workbench action, nor timeout may mark an incomplete Assessment complete or bypass remaining eligible work.
