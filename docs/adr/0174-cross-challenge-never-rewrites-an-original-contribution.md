# Cross-challenge never rewrites an original Contribution

After initial contributions freeze, the Engine may construct a bounded Challenge Package containing selected claims, Evidence references, conflicts, and explicit questions for a `challenge-analyst`. Its response may corroborate, dispute, identify a Proof Gap, or request further validation, but it cannot edit, delete, merge, or replace the original contribution or Finding candidate. Every challenge has its own identity, lineage, Evidence, uncertainty, and budget and remains distinguishable from the claim it examines.
