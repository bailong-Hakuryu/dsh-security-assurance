# Official exports require a SEALED Assessment

The canonical Assessment Bundle, every official Export Profile, and a Control Plane Submission can be generated only from a SEALED Assessment. BLOCKED and CANCELED Assessments may expose bounded, authorized Diagnostic Views in the Workbench for recovery and investigation, but those views are explicitly non-authoritative and cannot be downloaded, relabeled, submitted, or consumed as an Official Security Export.
