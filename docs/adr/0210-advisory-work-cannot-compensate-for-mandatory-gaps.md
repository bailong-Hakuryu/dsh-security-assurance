# Advisory work cannot compensate for mandatory gaps

Advisory obligations, optional Analyzers, speculative Role work, extra Findings, and best-effort breadth may enrich a report and discover blocking risk but cannot satisfy, downgrade, weight away, or hide a mandatory Coverage Obligation. Counts and coverage percentages are derived presentation metrics only. A mandatory `GAP` remains material under the Security Policy regardless of how much optional work completed elsewhere.
