# Default Policy blocks high risk and material uncertainty

The Baseline Policy Profile makes validated Critical and High Findings blocking, makes Medium Findings blocking when newly introduced or tied to a critical security invariant or externally reachable boundary, reports other Medium and all lower severities as non-blocking, and yields indeterminate when material Candidate Findings remain unresolved. The Strict Policy Profile blocks every validated Medium-or-higher Finding, and hosts may strengthen but not weaken the product safety ceiling.
