# Risk Decisions derive authority and Critical acceptance requires two

`recordRiskDecision` accepts Finding identity, decision, bounded rationale, declared compensating controls, expiry, expected Assessment revision, Contract Version, and idempotency key; every decision-maker identity and authority comes exclusively from the Security Invocation. A denial or non-break-glass acceptance may resolve its eligible decision step under Policy, while the first Critical break-glass approval creates only a pending immutable attestation. A second distinct qualified Authority must independently submit a matching decision before acceptance becomes effective, and no caller-supplied name can satisfy separation.
