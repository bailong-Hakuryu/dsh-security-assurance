# Candidate Overflow creates an explicit Coverage gap

The Coverage Plan freezes bounded Candidate admission quotas by Analyzer or Role, weakness family, component, phase, and Assessment, together with deterministic prioritization and backpressure rules. Reaching a limit never silently drops excess proposals or treats the processed subset as complete: the Service records Candidate Overflow, counts and source lineage where safely available, and creates an explicit Coverage Gap for the affected obligation. Mandatory Coverage containing unresolved overflow cannot support `SATISFIED`.
