# Risk Acceptance requires human or Control Plane Decision Authority

Models, role agents, analyzers, and ordinary service callers never possess Risk Acceptance authority. A Workbench decision requires Host-derived human Decision Authority, while Control Plane operation requires Decision Authority issued by the Control Plane Kernel and bound to its governed decision context. Accepting Critical risk through break-glass requires two distinct qualified human authorities, with both identities, rationale, scope, expiry, and authorization Evidence recorded.
