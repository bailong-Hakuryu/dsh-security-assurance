# Role Cards show governed lineage not chat authority

Codex-style Role Cards make specialist work observable by showing exact Role Definition, Attempt and parent lineage, lifecycle state, Provider and model summary, budget, named milestones, Evidence and Candidate counts, completion disposition, and Challenge relations. Cards do not present conversational confidence, typing animation, or a polished answer as authority and never imply that a Role approved the Assessment. Canonical Findings, Coverage, and Verdict remain visually and semantically distinct from Role activity.
