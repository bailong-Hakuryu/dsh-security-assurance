# Critical break-glass requires two independent Operator Invocations

After the first authorized Critical break-glass attestation, the Workbench shows a pending Dual Authority Attestation with its scope, rationale, controls, expiry, and first signer identity but no effective acceptance. Completion requires a second distinct qualified Operator to authenticate through an independently derived Security Invocation and submit a matching decision; the first session, an administrator impersonation field, browser state, shared capability, or repeated click cannot satisfy separation. Mismatch or expiry leaves the decision incomplete and auditable.
