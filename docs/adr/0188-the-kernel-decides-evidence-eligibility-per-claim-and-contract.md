# The Kernel decides Evidence Eligibility per Claim and Contract

Evidence existence, producer confidence, or prior use never makes it generally eligible. For every proposed use, the Assessment Kernel generates an Evidence Eligibility Decision binding the exact Security Claim, Validation Contract and version, Subject, Evidence identity and schema, producer qualification, execution class, protection and freshness, independence, and applicable negative controls. The decision is immutable and purpose-specific: one Evidence object may be eligible for one claim and ineligible for another without altering the underlying bytes.
