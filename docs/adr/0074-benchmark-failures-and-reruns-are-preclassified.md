# Benchmark failures and reruns are preclassified

Product timeout, budget exhaustion, crash, or incorrect outcome counts against its Evaluation Arm, while proven Benchmark Harness failure or Corpus corruption invalidates the complete Case for every compared Arm and triggers a uniform rerun. Selective reruns, best-run selection, and post-hoc failure reclassification are forbidden and every exclusion remains in the Evaluation Run Bundle.
