# Deep independent passes remain hidden from peers until frozen

During Deep independent analysis, the Workbench may show each pass's identity, budget, lifecycle, and non-semantic milestone to an authorized human but the execution channel never exposes peer outputs before each initial Contribution freezes. Human viewing cannot feed content back into a live peer, and the UI presents initial Contributions, subsequent Challenge Packages, and Evidence Convergence as separate phases after admission. It never collapses repeated outputs into one apparent consensus or lets display behavior weaken independence.
