# NOT_APPLICABLE requires eligible negative proof

An obligation resolves `NOT_APPLICABLE` only when qualified Subject Inventory, scope selection, and any required Negative Evidence prove that its precondition is absent from the frozen Subject. A producer assertion, empty tool result, unsupported parser, exclusion, timeout, or user checkbox cannot establish non-applicability. The Not Applicable Proof is linked to the exact selector and plan version, and later Plan Amendment facts that contradict it require a new resolution rather than silent reuse.
