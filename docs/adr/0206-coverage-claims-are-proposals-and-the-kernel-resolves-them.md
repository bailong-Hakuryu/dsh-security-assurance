# Coverage Claims are proposals and the Kernel resolves them

An Analyzer or Role Contribution may propose a Coverage Claim only for declared obligations and may attach Evidence Links, execution facts, limitations, and candidate results. Successful execution or the producer's assertion cannot satisfy coverage. The Kernel independently checks Result Admission, exact obligation completion rules, Provider and Analyzer Eligibility Decisions, Evidence eligibility, independence, Candidate Overflow, Proof Gaps, and terminal lineage before producing the authoritative Coverage Resolution.
