# Risk Acceptance occurs before Seal in a decision window

Risk Acceptance may be recorded only while an Assessment is BLOCKED in an explicit Risk Decision Window after the relevant Findings are established and before final Policy Evaluation and sealing. A decision cannot be appended to or used to rewrite a SEALED Assessment. Expiry or revocation does not mutate the historical Seal, but validation of a future Submission fails closed and requires a new Assessment or a new applicable decision under the then-current contract.
