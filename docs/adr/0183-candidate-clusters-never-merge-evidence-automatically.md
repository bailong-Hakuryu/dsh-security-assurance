# Candidate Clusters never merge Evidence automatically

An exact deterministic Finding Fingerprint may place admitted Candidates into a Candidate Cluster for review and validation efficiency, but clustering does not merge their claims, Evidence, provenance, uncertainty, or identities. Fuzzy similarity may propose a possible relationship only, and consolidation into one Finding requires recorded deterministic or governed adjudication that preserves every source and explains compatibility; contradictory candidates remain separate. One validated Finding may reference several supporting Candidates without allowing one Candidate's Evidence to silently support another.
