# Resource Proof measures limits, leaks, and recovery

Versioned large-Subject and pressure fixtures measure wall time, throughput, peak memory, CPU where available, database and Evidence growth, open handles, timers, process trees, long-poll concurrency, queue fairness, cancellation latency, restart reconciliation, and recovery duration for each supported platform and Profile. Thresholds and measurement method are frozen before qualification, and exhausted limits must yield the designed bounded or indeterminate outcome without corruption or leakage. Average duration alone is not Resource Proof.
