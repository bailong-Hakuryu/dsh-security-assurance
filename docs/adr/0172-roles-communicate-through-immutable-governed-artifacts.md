# Roles communicate through immutable governed artifacts

Role Agents do not share mutable memory, directly message one another, or participate in an unaudited group chat. Inter-role exchange occurs only through admitted immutable Role Contributions, published Evidence, bounded projections, Follow-up Requests, and Challenge Packages selected by the Assessment Engine. This preserves provenance and disclosure controls for every fact that can affect Coverage or a Finding while still allowing deliberate staged collaboration.
