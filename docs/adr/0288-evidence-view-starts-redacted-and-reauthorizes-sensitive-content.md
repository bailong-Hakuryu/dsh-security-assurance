# Evidence View starts redacted and reauthorizes sensitive content

The Evidence view initially renders classification, schema, producer lineage, Evidence Eligibility Decisions, Evidence Links, protection and retention status, size, digest, and a redacted summary. Opening sensitive source, full attack paths, transcripts, reproducer output, or other payload requires a new purpose-specific Service authorization and bounded Evidence View, with expiry and clear sensitivity labeling. The browser never receives an Evidence Store directory browser, durable decryption capability, or automatic preload of hidden content.
