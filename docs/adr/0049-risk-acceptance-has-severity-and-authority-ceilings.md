# Risk Acceptance has severity and authority ceilings

Baseline Policy does not accept Critical risk, requires an independent Security Authority, rationale, compensating controls, and short expiry for High risk, and requires an authorized reasoned expiring decision for lower severities. A separately enabled Break-glass Policy may permit Critical acceptance only through two distinct authorities and tightly bounded compensating controls; Agents, Analyzers, and Assessors cannot accept their own risk.
