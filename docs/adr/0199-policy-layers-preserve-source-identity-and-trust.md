# Policy Layers preserve source identity and trust

The product Baseline, authenticated Host or Organization Policy, and permitted Repository Strengthening Policy remain distinct Policy Layers with exact source identity, schema and content version, digest, authority, effective scope, and provenance. The canonical policy records both the layers and their composition result rather than retaining only a flattened document. A repository file is considered only from its frozen Subject location and never becomes trusted Policy merely because its filename resembles configuration.
