# Rejection requires contract-satisfying Counter-Evidence

A Candidate may be rejected only through Counter-Evidence and any Negative Controls required by its frozen Validation Contract, such as a proved unreachable path, incompatible dependency identity, enforced invariant, safe reproducer negative case, or disproved precondition. Merely failing to reproduce, failing to find a complete attack path, receiving a Reviewer opinion, or exhausting budget is not counter-proof and leaves the Candidate UNRESOLVED. Rejection retains the original Candidate and all unsuccessful validation Evidence.
